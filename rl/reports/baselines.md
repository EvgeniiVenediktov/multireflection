## Oracle

From the start tilt s, the reachable tilts are s + 0.01 deg * (i, j) inside the box; tilts reachable only by clamping at the +-2 deg limits are ignored. Moves per axis: exact BFS over the integers with the signed ladder steps (e.g. 29 = 30 - 1: 2 moves); a target costs max(moves_x, moves_y). Each episode scans its full shifted grid (401^2 states, float32 tilt arithmetic as in the env, steps ordered so that no intermediate tilt clamps) and takes argmax P / P_nom - 0.01 * moves, ties to fewer moves.

## Scripted and homing

`scripted` (image only, uses `obs['image']` and its own action history): M2 spot count = 3x3 local maxima >= 20/255; brightness = sum of pixels / 255. While <= 1 spot is visible, an expanding square spiral of 1.00 deg steps (legs 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, ...). From the first frame with >= 2 spots, a hill-climb on brightness over 8 directions with step sizes 1.00, 0.30 deg: a gain > 2 keeps the move and repeats the direction, a loss > 2 is reverted (one step), otherwise the move is kept and the next direction is tried; 8 probes without a gain shrink the step, after the last size it stops. Brightness instead of the spot count because the count saturates near 30 from 57 bounces up (spots overlap at 64 px), while brightness keeps rising: ~11 (one spot), ~144 (31), ~218 (57), ~250 (89), ~273 (trapped).

## Interpretation

The oracle returns 3.61 on average and goes to the trapped region (11.27 m) from 4095 of 4096 starts (stops at once: 1, of which 1 start inside it), in 2.8 moves (at most 5): the 0.2 deg trapped spot is a few ladder steps from anywhere in the box, so the 31 / 57 / 89 patterns are never worth stopping at for a fully informed agent. The image-only scripted policy returns 0.78 (22 % of the oracle, regret 2.83): it always ends at >= 31 bounces and at >= 57 in 55.0 %, but trapped in only 2.9 %, and spends 32 steps (0.31 of return in step costs); brightness is nearly constant within the 31 plateau (141-146) and the 57 island (216-219), so the 0.2 deg 89 / trapped spots are found only when a probe happens to land in them. For this heuristic the blind region is cheap: the 1 deg spiral reaches >= 31 bounces after 3.2 steps on average from blind starts, and the regret is 2.90 (blind) vs 2.78 (informative); the oracle needs 3.37 vs 2.46 moves (returns are higher from blind starts only because P_0 is lower). The image-free homing policy returns 3.58 (99 % of the oracle): with one fixed geometry the mount limit gives the absolute tilt (the clamp shows in obs['action'], and 4 pushes force it anyway), so stage 1 is solvable open-loop from a memorized map; PPO can learn exactly that, so stage-1 success alone does not show that the policy reads the images. Random policies have negative returns (-0.57 with stop, -0.72 without): step costs and wandering off the plateau outweigh the rare visits to long paths.
