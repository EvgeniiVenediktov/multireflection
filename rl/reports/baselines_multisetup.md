## Interpretation

With four setups the image-free homing policy that ignores the setup returns 0.28, 32 % of the oracle's 0.86 (single setup: 99 %): its one route ends at M2 (+0.60, -0.89), where the path is 0.24, 1.00, 0.26, 0.30 of P_max in measured, sep116, sep144, sep169 (per setup 8 %, 96 %, 10 %, 11 % of the oracle). Knowing the setup restores homing to 96 % (0.83): the gap is the value of identifying the setup, which an image-free agent cannot do and an image-reading agent can (the first M2 spot already differs between the setups, `setups.md`). The image-only scripted heuristic returns 0.08 (9 %), success 24.8 % vs the oracle's 100.0 %.
